Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for bookings, payments, and how to use WhereWeFishin features.

Who are we?

WhereWeFishin is an app for anglers who want to discover great spots, book pontoons quickly, and keep all useful information in one place.

Application goal

Our goal is to simplify the fishing experience through a clear, modern, and easy-to-use platform for users, spot managers, and administrators.

What you can do in WhereWeFishin

  • Find spots on the map and view location details.
  • Book pontoons and manage bookings from your profile.
  • Leave reviews and feedback for the community.
  • Use Fish Recognition to identify species.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a pontoon?

Open the target spot page, choose an available pontoon, select date and interval, then complete booking from the cart.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Depending on spot policy, you can cancel a booking from your profile, in the active bookings section.

What is Fish Recognition used for?

Upload a video clip with the fish and the system automatically identifies the species using the recognition model.

How can I view my booking history?

Open your profile page. In the bookings section you can find active and completed bookings.

Who can modify spot details?

Only authorized administrators and managers can edit spots, pontoons, and availability.

Do I need an account to book?

Yes, you need an account to complete bookings, view history, and receive updates related to your bookings.

How do I know if a pontoon is available?

On the spot page you can see real-time availability status for each pontoon, along with occupied intervals.

Can I book multiple pontoons at once?

Yes. You can add multiple bookings to the cart and complete them in one checkout flow.

Where can I see active bookings?

In your profile page, in the bookings section, where you can see each booking status, date, and selected interval.

How can I become a lake manager?

Any registered user can submit a manager application directly from the app. Fill in the lake details, mark the location on the map, and describe your motivation. An administrator will review and approve your request. Apply here →

What should I do if payment was not confirmed?

First check your profile history. If the booking is not listed, try again or contact the spot administrator with transaction details.

Can I use the app on my phone?

Yes, WhereWeFishin is optimized for mobile, so you can search spots, book, and check bookings directly from the browser.

How do I add a review for a spot?

After a completed booking, open the spot page and fill in the review form with rating and comment.

Who can manage spots in the platform?

Spots are managed by validated administrators and managers, who can add, update, or remove details depending on role.

What file types are accepted for Fish Recognition?

Clear video clips with the fish visible in frame are recommended for the most accurate recognition results.

How do I contact the WhereWeFishin team?

You can send feedback from the app through reviews or contact spot managers directly from the spot page.
